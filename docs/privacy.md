# Dudu 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

更新于 2026-11-12 · Last updated 2026-11-12

## 中文

Dudu（iPhone App）不收集你的数据。开发者没有服务器，App 里没有统计、广告和跟踪，也没有联网的第三方 SDK。

**数据去哪儿。** App 只和你配对的那台电脑通信（局域网或你自己设置的地址）。对话、事项、图片、文件和语音转成的文字，只在手机和你的电脑之间传输。你电脑上的 DSH 和 Agent 怎样把内容发给模型服务商，由你在电脑上的设置决定，不经过本 App 的任何服务器。

**手机上保存什么。** 配对凭据（存在系统钥匙串）、最近打开的对话缓存、没发出的草稿，以及界面诊断日志（记录点击耗时、请求耗时、错误等，不记录你输入的文字），都只保存在手机上，不会上传。删除 App 会清除缓存和日志。

**权限。** 相机：扫描配对二维码、给消息拍照。麦克风和语音识别：按住说话，转成文字发送；设备支持时在设备上识别，不支持时由 Apple 的语音服务处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。照片：选择图片作为附件，以及把对话里的图片保存到照片图库。本地网络：连接局域网里的电脑。每项权限都只在你使用对应功能时才会请求。

**儿童。** Dudu 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。

**变更与联系。** 政策有变化会更新本页。问题请到 https://github.com/weiwenhao/dudu/issues 反馈。

## English

The Dudu iPhone app does not collect your data. The developer runs no server, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDK that uses the network.

**Where data goes.** The app only talks to the computer you paired (on your local network or at an address you set). Conversations, issues, pictures, files and speech converted to text travel only between your phone and your computer. How DSH and the agents on your computer send content to model providers is up to your settings there; it does not pass through any server of this app.

**What stays on the phone.** The pairing credential (in the system Keychain), a cache of recently opened conversations, unsent drafts, and interface diagnostics logs (tap and request timings, errors; not the text you type) are stored on the phone only and are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes the cache and the logs.

**Permissions.** Camera: scan the pairing QR code, take a picture for a message. Microphone and Speech Recognition: hold to talk, converted to text and sent; recognition runs on the device when it is supported, otherwise Apple's speech service handles it under Apple's privacy policy. Photos: choose pictures as attachments, and save a picture from a conversation to the photo library. Local Network: reach the computer on your network. Each permission is requested only when you use the matching feature.

**Children.** Dudu is not directed to children under 13.

**Changes and contact.** This page is updated when the policy changes. Questions: https://github.com/weiwenhao/dudu/issues
